Our Idea

The problem statement was an open-ended challenge aimed at showcasing our AI/ML skills by solving a real-world problem.

We chose to address one of the most impactful domains — agriculture — by building an intelligent crop recommendation system for farmers. Our solution leverages machine learning to analyse soil parameters, climate conditions, and environmental data to suggest the most suitable crops for a given region or field. To ensure accessibility, we developed both a web-based platform and a desktop application, enabling farmers and stakeholders to easily interact with the system across different environments.
